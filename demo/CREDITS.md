Music: "Gear" by Andrew Ev, from Mixkit (track 180, https://mixkit.co/free-stock-music/).

TV or radio broadcast, or video games; no remixing into a music-only track. No credit is required.

Edit (cinetic's `track.py edit --map "1:103x2,3:105x8,11:120x8" --end own`): 18 bars at 127 BPM, source bars
103-112 then 120-127, one splice on a bar line, the track's own final hit and ring-out under the end card.
The picture's cuts sit on its beats (`demo/film.mjs`).

Effects: a click on each click and a short swish on the two scene changes, rendered from earshot's sound bank
(`click/soft`, made in code; `fit/whoosh.short/bamboo_swish`, fitted from a CC0 recording). They are not recordings.

Mix: about -15 LUFS, true peak under -1.5 dB. The audio files are not kept in this repository.

An earlier, slower cut used "Digital Clouds" by Alejandro Magaña (Mixkit track 175, same licence).
